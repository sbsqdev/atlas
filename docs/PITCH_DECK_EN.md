# Human Atlas — Pitch Deck (slide-by-slide)

A fitness ed-tech platform: trainers publish courses, students follow them on a
real 3D muscle map, and an Atomic-Habits engine (goals, streaks, friends, clans)
keeps them coming back. Trilingual (RU/EN/TR). Live demo: www.fab3d-scan.com

---

## Slide 1 — Title / Hook
- **Human Atlas** — "Train with awareness. Reach your goal."
- One line: The 3D-muscle training platform that actually makes people finish.
- Logo, URL, contact. (Optional subline: trainer courses + Atomic Habits.)

## Slide 2 — Problem
- 80% of people who start training quit within weeks.
- They don't *see* what they're working or their progress → motivation dies.
- Trainers sell programs over chat/PDF with no way to see who's actually doing them.
- Generic fitness apps are impersonal; coaching is personal but doesn't scale.

## Slide 3 — Solution
- A platform where a **trainer gives a code**, the student joins (like Google
  Classroom), and trains on a **real 3D muscle map** that shows exactly what each
  exercise works — wrapped in a **psychology-based habit engine** that makes
  people *want* to come back.

## Slide 4 — Product (what it does)
- **3D muscle atlas** (BodyParts3D): per-exercise primary/secondary highlighting.
- **Goals & habits** (Atomic Habits: 4 laws + identity): streaks, "Today" card,
  completion celebration.
- **Profile** with saved stats + activity heatmap → people return and feel proud.
- **Social**: friends, "together effect," **clans**, per-training chat + video circles.
- **Trainer tools**: build courses, assign muscles (AI-assisted), see who did what.

## Slide 5 — Why now
- Post-pandemic home-training is permanent; creators monetize communities.
- 3D web graphics + open anatomy data are finally production-ready in the browser.
- Behavioral science (Atomic Habits) is mainstream — retention is the real moat.

## Slide 6 — Market
- TAM: global fitness app market (~$10B+, double-digit CAGR).
- SAM: online coaching / creator-led fitness (trainers + their audiences).
- SOM (wedge): Russian-/Turkic-speaking trainers and their clients first (underserved, trilingual).
- Bottom-up: # of trainers × avg clients × ARPU.

## Slide 7 — Business model
- **Trainer subscription** (SaaS): publish courses, analytics, branding.
- **Take-rate / Pro tier** on paid courses (payments handled by trainer today).
- **Premium for students**: advanced stats, clans, extra challenges.
- Low COGS: static front end + serverless; scales per trainer.

## Slide 8 — Go-to-market
- Land trainers 1-by-1 (they bring their own clients → built-in distribution).
- Each trainer's invite codes = viral loop into the app.
- Content: Reels/Telegram promos, "12-workout challenge," referral via friends/clans.
- Expand language by language (RU → TR → EN).

## Slide 9 — Traction / status
- **Live MVP shipped** (www.fab3d-scan.com): 3D atlas, courses, goals, habits,
  profile, friends, clans, chat, video circles — trilingual, dark sporty UI.
- First trainer onboarded (Rauana Training, glutes & legs program).
- *(Fill in:* users, active trainers, D7/D30 retention, streak rates.*)*

## Slide 10 — Competition / moat
- vs. generic apps (Nike, Freeletics): we add **trainer-owned courses + real 3D**.
- vs. course platforms (Teachable): we add **3D anatomy + habit retention engine**.
- vs. spreadsheets/Telegram coaching: structure, progress, accountability.
- **Moat**: retention via habit loops + social (clans) + trainer lock-in (their audience & content live here).

## Slide 11 — Roadmap
- Now: shared **backend** (accounts, courses, progress sync across devices).
- Next: real cross-person social (friends/clans/chat/circles), payments, Telegram Web App + reminders.
- Later: AI coach (form tips, auto-programming), marketplace of trainers, wearables.
