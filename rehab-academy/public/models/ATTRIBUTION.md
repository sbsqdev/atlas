## knee.glb

Subset of 18 right lower-limb structures around the knee
(Right femur; Right patella; Right tibia; Right fibula; Right rectus femoris; Right vastus lateralis; Right vastus medialis; Right vastus intermedius; Long head of right biceps femoris; Short head of right biceps femoris; Right semitendinosus; Right semimembranosus; Medial head of right gastrocnemius; Lateral head of right gastrocnemius; Right popliteus; Right sartorius; Right gracilis; Right iliotibial tract),
re-centred on the patella, normals dequantized, packed as glTF 2.0.

Not included (no separate meshes exist in BodyParts3D 4.0): cruciate and collateral ligaments, menisci, patellar ligament, quadriceps tendon.
The app lists these as structures without a licensed 3D asset.
